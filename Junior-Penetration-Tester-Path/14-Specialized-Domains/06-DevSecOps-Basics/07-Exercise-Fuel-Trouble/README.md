# 07 - Exercise: Fuel Trouble

## Overview

This exercise compares three software development models:

    Waterfall
    Agile
    DevOps

The scenario uses a fictional space mission to demonstrate how each model approaches decision-making and change.

---

## Mission

Travel to the planet with the least amount of risk possible.

### Available Fuel

    130,000 Light Years

### Planets

| Planet | Distance |
|---|---:|
| Testooine | 125,000 LY |
| Hackboo | 100,000 LY |
| TryHothMe | 80,000 LY |
| Dagobug | 60,000 LY |

---

# Comic 1 - Waterfall

The initial decision is made to travel to Testooine.

Tests are performed and the original decision remains the chosen course.

The approach represents a sequential model where the plan is established and followed.

### Answer

    Waterfall

---

# Comic 2 - Agile

Tests indicate that Testooine is high risk.

The team changes its decision and adjusts the course based on new information and testing.

The approach demonstrates:

    Flexibility
    Adaptability
    Responding to Change

### Answer

    Agile

---

# Comic 3 - DevOps

The initial decision is to travel to Hoth.

Costs are questioned.

SEC3PO analyses the trajectory and changes testing parameters.

Chewba-QA performs further testing and the team changes the orbit to Dagobah.

The process demonstrates:

    Collaboration
    Continuous Testing
    Shared Visibility
    Automation / Integration
    Continuous Change

### Answer

    DevOps

---

## Software Development Models

### Waterfall

    Plan
      ↓
    Execute
      ↓
    Test
      ↓
    Deliver

### Agile

    Plan
      ↓
    Develop
      ↓
    Test
      ↓
    Learn
      ↓
    Adapt
      ↓
    Repeat

### DevOps

    Development
      ↕
    Testing
      ↕
    Operations
      ↕
    Automation
      ↕
    Continuous Feedback

# Actions on Objectives

## Overview

This final operational phase represents the attacker's ultimate goal. Depending on the campaign, objectives may include data theft, financial gain, espionage, service disruption, or system destruction.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand attacker objectives
- Identify potential business impacts
- Relate technical compromise to organizational risk

## Common Objectives

- Data Exfiltration
- Credential Theft
- Ransomware Deployment
- Lateral Movement
- Service Disruption

## Skills Practiced

- Impact Assessment
- Threat Modeling
- Risk Analysis

## Key Takeaways

- Every previous phase exists to achieve the attacker's final objective.
- Business impact often exceeds the technical impact.
- Early detection prevents attackers from reaching this stage.

### Human-Centered Attacks

Unlike traditional exploits, phishing targets people instead of software vulnerabilities.

Successful attacks often rely on:

- Trust
- Urgency
- Authority
- Fear
- Curiosity
- Scarcity

Understanding these principles helps penetration testers create realistic simulations while helping organizations improve user awareness.

---

### Technical Techniques

The room also covered several technical phishing methods, including:

- URL masking
- Typosquatting
- Homograph attacks
- Email spoofing
- Credential harvesting
- Malicious document delivery

These techniques increase the realism and effectiveness of phishing campaigns.

---

### Ethical Phishing

Professional phishing engagements must always:

- Be explicitly authorized
- Follow agreed Rules of Engagement
- Remain within scope
- Avoid harming users or production systems
- Focus on improving organizational security rather than compromising users

The objective is education, risk assessment, and continuous improvement.

## Key Takeaways

- Phishing remains one of the most effective methods for obtaining initial access during a penetration test.
- Successful phishing attacks combine psychological manipulation with technical deception.
- Campaigns should follow a structured process including planning, reconnaissance, execution, and reporting.
- Tools such as the Social Engineering Toolkit (SET) help simulate realistic phishing attacks in controlled environments.
- Ethical phishing assessments improve organizational security by identifying weaknesses in user awareness and strengthening defenses against social engineering.

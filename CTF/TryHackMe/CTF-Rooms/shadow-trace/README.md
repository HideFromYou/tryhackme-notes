# Shadow Trace - TryHackMe

## Overview

Shadow Trace is an OSINT-focused challenge that introduces practical techniques for gathering publicly available information from multiple online sources. The room develops investigation skills by encouraging a structured approach to collecting, validating, and correlating digital evidence without directly interacting with the target.

## Objectives

* Perform open-source intelligence (OSINT) investigations.
* Collect publicly available information.
* Correlate findings from multiple sources.
* Validate gathered intelligence.
* Complete the investigation objectives.

## Skills Learned

* Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)
* Digital footprint analysis
* Information correlation
* Search engine techniques
* Online investigation methodology
* Evidence validation

## Investigation

The investigation begins by identifying publicly available information related to the target. Multiple online resources are examined and correlated to build a clearer picture while filtering out misleading or irrelevant information. Careful validation ensures that conclusions are based on reliable evidence.

## Analysis

Collected intelligence is analysed to identify meaningful relationships between people, organisations, domains, usernames, or other digital artefacts. The investigation demonstrates how seemingly unrelated pieces of public information can be combined to support an assessment.

## Key Takeaways

* Effective OSINT relies on methodology rather than luck.
* Always verify information using multiple independent sources.
* Small publicly available details can reveal valuable intelligence.
* Careful documentation improves investigation quality and repeatability.

## Tools Used

* Search Engines
* WHOIS
* DNS Lookup
* Social Media Platforms
* Browser Developer Tools
